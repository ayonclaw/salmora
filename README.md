# Salmora

Salmora is a browser game about a lone salvage diver working the flooded ruins of a drowned city: you swim through sunken streets, cut open wrecks for scrap, dodge the things that followed the water in, and haul your finds back to the surface before the air runs out. It is being built for the make.fun competition `gta6-01` — a $1,666 prize pool paid across the top 100 places, with participation credit for the top 1000 — and everything here is written from scratch inside the competition window.
